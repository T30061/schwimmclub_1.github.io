About the club

A new generation of swim club energy.

We combine technical coaching, inclusive community, and a strong athlete mindset to help every swimmer move forward with purpose.

Built around progression, confidence, and belonging.

ASKÖ Swimclub Neusiedl is designed for swimmers who want more than a lane: they want a place where technique improves, confidence grows, and the club feels energising every time they arrive.

Our culture blends performance discipline with warmth and encouragement, creating a balanced environment where juniors, adults, and competitors all move forward together.

  • Technical excellence
  • Inclusive group culture
  • Authentic club identity
  • Measurable growth
